A next-generation three-route Avenrà EVO browser game with Living Roads, Rider Dynamics 2.0, Weekly Works Runs, opt-in route leaderboards and offline play.

== Description ==

Avenrà Hyperlane adds a complete 90-second first-person motorcycle game to WordPress.

The plugin includes:

* A premium, responsive landing page created automatically on activation.
* The complete game bundled locally inside the plugin.
* Three ride modes: 60 mph, 90 mph and 109 mph.
* HyperCore Boost up to 132 mph in every mode.
* Four road conditions: Dry Day, Raining Day, Dry Night and Raining Night.
* One performance-refined Ultra graphics profile on every route, with an internal resolution that self-tunes to the device.
* A guided three-step pre-ride setup for Route, Conditions and the final Ready review.
* A bounded Preparing Your EVO stage that draws and calibrates the selected scene before a true three-second countdown.
* Three complete routes: City (Intermediate), Rural (Hardest) and M1 Motorway (Easiest), all upgraded to the next-generation visual and traffic systems.
* A dynamic Living Roads director built from deterministic 80–120 m entrance, interchangeable middle and exit chapters, with four to six chapters streamed around the rider, concealed transitions, non-overlapping route-authentic situations and fair advance warnings.
* A UK Traffic Brain with keep-left/overtake-right lane advice and a cruise > follow > brake > signal > reserve gap > change lane > settle state flow. Traffic responds to the motorcycle, surrounding vehicles, road layout and current conditions, while the established route director retains authority over physical lane movement.
* A five-part Rider Rating and post-ride debrief covering Pace, Precision, Awareness, Smoothness and Discipline.
* A Weekly Works Run that gives every rider the same route, weather and traffic seed for a directly comparable challenge.
* Rider Dynamics 2.0 with an optional deeper handling model while preserving the accessible original ride experience.
* A full-screen EVO Rider route home with clipped cinematic M1, A66 and A40 cards, UK route shields, route-specific personal bests and accessible keyboard navigation. It decorates the existing setup controls rather than duplicating their actions.
* The original two-way City course through the Avenrà District, HALO Tunnel and Racing Expressway.
* A shared 1.28 m sports-bike rider eye point across every route and graphics tier for a lower, more convincing first-person view.
* A Living Roads Yorkshire Dales run with distinct Ribblehead, Hawes and Buttertubs Pass road character, substantially heavier oncoming traffic and two short signed 2+1 overtaking sections.
* Continuous perspective-scaled Rural scenery on both verges, moving from low Ribblehead dry-stone boundaries through lusher Wensleydale hedges to open Buttertubs grass, heather and rock banks while preserving overtaking sightlines.
* Rebalanced Rural horizon composition and crest occlusion driven by the same rider-camera geometry.
* Authentic Rural road language including changing centre lines, SLOW legends, cattle grids, farm mud, drainage and height-aware crest visibility.
* A more believable City with correct kerbs, footways, gutters, drains and double-yellow lines, plus active crossings, loading and bus-stop scenes.
* HALO Tunnel safety equipment and operational tunnel and Expressway message signs alongside the Avenrà campaign boards.
* A compressed M1 Northbound Motorway run from Luton to Leeds, featuring ordered destination signs and Toddington, Watford Gap, Woodall and Woolley Edge services.
* A playable Motorway hard shoulder for emergency avoidance, with HALO road-discipline deductions only when it is used to complete an overtake.
* A warned Midlands roadworks set-piece with a lane-three closure, merging traffic, cone taper, temporary studs, red-X signal and recoverable lane-breach penalty.
* UK-style red, white and amber cat's eyes in every Motorway graphics profile.
* Route-specific night lighting: a continuously lit City with warm District sodium and cool Expressway LED, an authentically unlit M1 carrying high-mast lighting only around junctions and services, and an unlit Rural run broken by short village sections and set-back farmhouse spill.
* Restrained dusk, night and poor-visibility halos anchored to the rendered heads of street and high-mast lights and to each photographic vehicle's active lamps, while the established renderer remains the single owner of every physical source and road reflection.
* World-windscreen rain water with speed-driven airflow, softened trails and optional local refraction, with tunnel, impact, viewport and Reduced Motion handling.
* Extended Motorway road language: 300, 200 and 100 yard countdown markers, blue driver location signs, 100 metre marker posts, orange emergency refuge areas with SOS marking, average-speed camera pairs and chevron hatching in every merge nose.
* Extended City road language: pedestrian crossing zig-zags, bus lanes, mini roundabouts, advanced stop lines for cyclists, high-friction junction approaches and Gatso camera sites.
* Extended Rural road language: centre hatching widening into ghost islands, signed passing places, national speed limit derestriction, a level crossing and worn, grass-bitten carriageway edges.
* Continuous perspective-scaled photographic Motorway verges on both sides of the M1, moving from Luton grass, fencing and screening through denser Midlands woodland to Yorkshire fields, hedges and mature trees.
* Sparse, persistent Motorway buildings and service-area landmarks set behind the vegetation instead of alternating empty space with sudden structures.
* Human-like traffic hazards: telegraphed phantom braking, poorly judged overtakes and vehicles emerging from District side roads.
* Physical traffic separation that prevents overtaking, merging and opposing AI vehicles from passing through each other.
* Oil spills, large puddles and potholes with recoverable handling effects and HALO Focus Zone warnings.
* A substantially larger EVO rider cockpit with mirrors, forearms and gloves, plus a stable rider eye line and bike movement beneath the camera.
* Native-aspect photographic calibration for each route, including stored horizon, vanishing point, road-corner alignment and safe crop limits, plus separate phone, tablet and desktop portrait/landscape framing. Peripheral crop and overscan preserve road geometry; images are never stretched to fill the viewport.
* Colour-matched distance haze independent of the weather selection, with Clear, Rain and Night depth treatment; near-field roadside motion, depth-aware traffic detail and selective edge blur remain route-aware.

Additional UK fleet profiles cover estates, taxis, buses, coaches, caravans, horseboxes, delivery vans and articulated HGVs using the nearest intact source sprite with vehicle-specific dimensions and markings.
* EVO cockpit instrumentation and live telemetry.

* A guided three-step HALO Emergency Assist operator challenge after a collision, with a clear role handoff and game-menu exit.
* Installable, offline-ready core game after the first successful load, with visual and audio assets cached on demand as they are used.
* Desktop keyboard, mobile tap/drag and optional tilt steering.

No Node server, external game engine or player account is required. Personal bests and preferences stay in the visitor's browser. Players may separately opt in to publish a rider tag and their best safely banked checkpoint or completed-route statistics to the WordPress-hosted leaderboard.

HALO is a fictional training simulation in this game. It does not place calls, contact Avenrà Emergency Assist or dispatch emergency services.

== Installation ==

1. In WordPress, open Plugins > Add New Plugin > Upload Plugin.
2. Select the `avenra-hyperlane-3.3.15.zip` file and choose Install Now.
3. Activate Avenrà Hyperlane.
4. The plugin creates and publishes an `Avenrà Hyperlane: The Game` page automatically.
5. Open Plugins > Installed Plugins and choose `View Hyperlane`, or add the new page to your website menu.

If another page already uses the `avenra-hyperlane` address, WordPress gives the new managed page a unique address without changing the existing page.

== How to Play ==

1. Follow the Route and Conditions steps, then review the complete run on Ready.
2. Select City, Rural or Motorway. City is Intermediate, Rural is Hardest and Motorway is Easiest.
3. Choose one of four road conditions: Dry Day, Raining Day, Dry Night or Raining Night. Every ride uses the single performance-refined Ultra graphics profile.
4. On Ready, select Ride Mode 1, 2 or 3 and start the ride.
5. Watch for HALO Focus Zone warnings, then steer around oil, standing water and potholes.
6. Pass traffic cleanly to build Flow and increase the score multiplier.
7. Hold Boost to use maximum HyperCore performance.
8. Clear each checkpoint to bank the score, then finish the course for a full-route result.
9. On Motorway, obey lane-closure warnings. The hard shoulder can save a collision, but completing an overtake there deducts 650 points and resets Flow.

Desktop controls:

* Left Arrow or A: steer left.
* Right Arrow or D: steer right.
* Down Arrow or S: brake.
* Hold Space: HyperCore Boost.
* 1, 2 or 3: change ride mode.

Mobile controls:

* Tap left or right, or drag across the game, to steer.
* Hold the on-screen Brake or Boost controls.
* Select Enable Tilt and approve motion access for optional tilt steering.

== Offline Play ==

On an HTTPS website, open the game using `Open Game` or `Open Full Screen`, then select `Install for Offline`. Allow the initial load to finish before disconnecting. The core game page and its linked launch dependencies are then available offline; visual and audio assets cache on demand as they are used instead of all being downloaded during installation. Browser installation support varies, and the game remains playable normally when installation is unavailable.

== Shortcodes ==

`[avenra_hyperlane]`

Renders the complete landing page content inside a theme page.

`[avenra_hyperlane_game]`

Renders only the isolated game frame. An optional height may be supplied, for example `[avenra_hyperlane_game height="800"]`.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does the game depend on the ChatGPT-hosted demo? =

No. Version 3.3.15 packages the game code, cockpit artwork, campaign imagery, graphics packs, icons and offline worker inside the plugin.

= Does HALO make a real emergency call? =

No. The HALO sequence is explicitly marked as an in-game simulation before and throughout the challenge. It makes no network request, phone call or emergency-service dispatch.

= What happens to the landing page if I deactivate the plugin? =

The WordPress page is preserved so administrator-created content is never silently deleted. Reactivate the plugin to restore the game and cinematic template.

= Can I place the game on a different page? =

Yes. Use either shortcode in the WordPress editor. Removing `[avenra_hyperlane]` from the automatically generated page also releases that page back to the active theme.

== Privacy ==

The plugin has no analytics and does not link leaderboard entries to WordPress accounts. Personal best, selected conditions, graphics preference, a random private game key and offline files are stored locally by the visitor's browser.

The public leaderboard is optional. Only after explicit consent, the game sends the chosen rider tag, selected route and the statistics captured at the rider's furthest safely completed checkpoint or the full route to this WordPress website. The server keeps one best result per route and season and stores a one-way hash of the random game key so the same browser can update or remove its entries; it does not store the raw key, email address, WordPress username or account ID. Short-lived one-way request fingerprints are used only for abuse prevention. Collision details, HALO incident evidence and operator decisions are not published.

Deleting the plugin permanently removes its leaderboard table. Deactivation and normal plugin updates preserve it.
